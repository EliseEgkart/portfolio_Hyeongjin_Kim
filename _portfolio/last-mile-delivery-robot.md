## Contributions

- Computed encoder-based odometry from in-wheel motors and integrated Cartographer SLAM.
- Implemented goal-directed navigation, vision-based elevator button recognition, and IMU-based floor-transition detection.
- Integrated firmware, electronics, software, and mechanical fabrication, including welding and painting.
