Research themes

Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Systems

Learning-based high-level decisions in dynamic environments and robotic systems, including path planning on distributed IoT edge platforms.

Related work: Split federated learning and deep Q-networks

Networked and Distributed Intelligence

AI-enabled IoT, distributed learning, and V2X-enabled systems. Research interests include decision-making under communication, sensing, and resource constraints.

Related work: Journal articles and KICS presentations

Robot Learning and Manipulation

Research interests in robot learning and robotic manipulation, informed by hands-on experience connecting perception, navigation, and pick-and-place manipulation in ROS 2 systems.

Related systems: RoboCup mobile manipulator · Delivery robot · Smart stroller

UAV-Assisted Intelligent Systems

Published work on sensing, surveillance, and networking using unmanned aerial vehicles, with a focus on resource allocation and autonomous decision-making.

Related work: Resting-drone surveillance · ITS coverage and V2X
